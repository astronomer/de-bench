# Ticket NW-214: which customer count is real?

Finance and the customer team have brought two different active trade-account numbers to the
FY2026 planning meeting three weeks running. The Monday board pack says 5,340. The CRM export
the customer team pulls says 5,092. Both were pulled the same morning. The two have never
matched, and the revenue-per-account line in the plan moves five percent depending on which
one we use.

Planning needs one number, and a method behind it that holds up when the auditors ask where
the number came from. Nobody has ever written that method down.

Work it out from the account books themselves. They are the record of who our trade accounts
are, and everything downstream of them is what we are trying to explain rather than something
to take on trust. Copperline's own accounts and the accounts that came over with the Northwave
acquisition are all ours, so the answer is one number for the whole company, covering both
books.

Write it up in `RESPONSE.md` at the root of the working tree. Four things go in it:

- the count, on a line of its own, written `Active trade accounts: <number>`
- how that count splits across the two books — one figure for each, and the two add to the count
- how many accounts sit on both books, and where the pairing came from
- what the board pack's 5,340 and the CRM's 5,092 are each counting, and where each of them
  differs from the number you land on

Keep it short. Finance pastes it into the plan.

This is a question, not a change. Planning closes at the end of the week and nothing in the
pipelines moves until it does.
